## 1 · How the company earns money — 3 minutes

Coca-Cola owns beverage brands and sells concentrates, syrups and finished products. Independent bottling partners manufacture and distribute many drinks. Which activities earn the most margin and require the most physical assets?

Read the **FY2025 Form 10-K** (year ended **31 December 2025**) and **Q3 2026 Form 10-Q** (quarter ended **2 October 2026**). Start with **Item 1 — Business**, **Item 8 — Financial Statements**, and the quarterly **Part I, Item 1** statements.

**Calculate:** Gross margin = gross profit / revenue; operating margin = operating income / revenue. Do changing margins tell a different story from revenue growth?

## 3 · Balance sheet — 5 minutes

Compare FY2025 and FY2024 balances for *cash*, *property, plant and equipment*, *goodwill* and *trademarks with indefinite lives*.

**Calculate:** Property, plant and equipment / total assets. Compare physical assets with intangible assets. Does a brand's accounting carrying value necessarily equal its economic or market value?

## 5 · Connect the statements — 2 minutes

Imagine Coca-Cola transfers one of its bottling factories to an independent bottler but continues selling concentrates to that partner. Predict the possible changes in **revenue**, **operating margin**, **physical assets** and **future capital expenditure**.

> **Key concept:** Revenue scale and capital efficiency are not the same thing.
