## 1 · How the company earns money — 3 minutes

BHP produces iron ore, copper and other commodities for industrial customers, including steel producers and smelters. Mines require large investments in equipment and infrastructure, while commodity prices are largely set by global supply and demand.

Find *property, plant and equipment*, *cash*, *interest-bearing liabilities* and *rehabilitation provisions*.

**Calculate:** Property, plant and equipment / total assets. Compare BHP's stated net debt across FY2025 and FY2026 using its definition. How could future mine closure obligations affect valuation despite minimal current cash outflow?

## 4 · Cash flow statement — 5 minutes

Find cash from operating activities, spending on property and equipment, asset-sale proceeds, borrowing and repayments, and dividends.

**Calculate:** Operating cash flow less cash capital expenditure. Compare this simple figure with BHP's own free cash flow reconciliation. Do the definitions match, and how much cash remains after dividends?

## 5 · Connect the statements — 2 minutes

Suppose BHP spends $5 billion developing a mine several years before production starts. Which financial statement records the cash outflow first? When does depreciation begin, and why might free cash flow decline during a promising expansion?

> **Key concept:** Operating leverage and capital intensity make mining profits highly sensitive to commodity cycles and investment timing.
