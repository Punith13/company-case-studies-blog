## 1 · How the company earns money — 3 minutes

Amazon sells products online, provides services to third-party sellers and advertisers, sells subscriptions, and delivers cloud computing through AWS. Shoppers, sellers, advertisers and cloud customers pay for very different things.

Read the **FY2025 Form 10-K** (year ended **31 December 2025**) and **Q3 2026 Form 10-Q** (quarter ended **30 September 2026**). Locate disclosures for the **North America**, **International** and **AWS** operating segments.

## 3 · Balance sheet — 5 minutes

Find *cash*, *inventories*, *accounts receivable and other*, *accounts payable*, *property and equipment*, and *long-term debt* as of December 2025 and September 2026.

**Calculate:** Current assets − current liabilities. Compare accounts payable with inventory. Can customer payments arrive before supplier payments are due? What happens if inventory turnover slows?

## 5 · Connect the statements — 4 minutes

AWS buys $10 billion of servers for cash, depreciated straight-line over five years with no residual value. Predict the effects on cash, property and equipment, investing cash flow, free cash flow, annual depreciation and future operating profit.

What increase in future demand would make the purchase worthwhile?

> **Key concept:** Good capital allocation depends on the future cash returns produced by today's investments.
